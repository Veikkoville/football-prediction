What is GoalIQ?

GoalIQ is a free FPL assistant and football prediction tool, in the app stores as GoalIQ: FPL Assistant. Free FPL tools: clean sheet probability and fixture difficulty with separate attack and defence views, a player lookup with official availability news, start chance, set-piece roles, price and ownership, rate my team with a captain pick, a mini-league view with head-to-head win probability, a fit checker and pre-season draft rater with saved drafts, price watch, a watchlist for the players you are deciding on (three on the free plan), Beat the model: log your captain and transfer calls before the deadline and see them graded against the model's every gameweek, and the season race against the model's own squad, which is locked before every deadline and scored with official FPL points, and Catch your rival: how likely you are to close the gap to anyone in your mini-league with the gameweeks that are left. GoalIQ Premium adds an interactive team manager with a GW1 to GW6 gameweek planner, player expected points (xP) with ownership, minutes outlook, the per 90 minute rate and CSV export (web), the captain ranker, chip timing, transfer plan chains, an edge mode for your mini-league, transfer suggestions, player value, a DefCon tracker with a gameweek-by-gameweek breakdown, a 50-player watchlist and differentials. It can also predict any match: the app uses a Dixon-Coles statistical model to estimate match outcomes, expected goals (xG), and likely scorelines.

GoalIQ is not a betting platform. It is an analytics tool for football fans who want to understand match probabilities. Predictions are statistical estimates, never guarantees.

How accurate are the predictions?

Predictions are probabilistic, not deterministic. A 60 % home-win probability means that, across many similar matches, the home team would win roughly 60 % of the time. It does not mean this specific match will be a home win.

The underlying model is fitted on historical match results and validated against past seasons. We tune it continuously as more data arrives.
