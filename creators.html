GoalIQ creator program

You get a discount code and a link for your audience, and 30 percent of what they pay on the site. That's the rate for as long as they stay subscribed.

What you get

  • A code with your name on it. Your audience gets 30 percent off their first payment. It works on every plan at the web checkout.
  • 30 percent of what they pay. On the season pass that is 5.25 euros on the first year and 7.50 euros on every renewal after it.
  • Attribution that survives the discount. The code is written into the subscription when the payment goes through, so renewals stay attributed to you even though the discount itself only applies once.
  • Your own page for your own numbers. Sign in at pro.goaliq.app/creator with the account we linked your code to. It shows how many accounts carry your code and how many subscriptions are credited to you. Nobody else can see them, and you can't see anyone else's.
  • No exclusivity and no quota. Promote whatever else you want.

What you earn

Commission is 30 percent of the euro price the buyer pays. GoalIQ uses Stripe Adaptive Pricing, so a reader in the UK sees pounds and a reader in the US sees dollars; your commission is still counted in euros. The season pass is 25 euros a year on our front page, and your code takes 30 percent off the first payment, which is where the 17.50 comes from.

What we ask

  • Say it is an affiliate link. Whatever your platform calls it, mark it. That one isn't negotiable.
  • Only use numbers a reader can check. Everything on goaliq.app that sits outside the paywall is fair game. Don't quote figures from behind it.
  • Don't promise results. The model is graded in public and it misses. Point people at the record and let them decide.

The small print

  • Until 12 September. Premium is free on the web until the GW4 deadline, so most people have no reason to pay yet and your code will probably sit at zero until the window closes. The checkout stays open the whole time though, and if someone does buy before then it counts and gets paid like any other sale.
  • When you get paid. Commission builds up from your first referral. The first payouts go out at gameweek 19, against your invoice. If invoicing is awkward for you, say so when you apply and we'll sort something else.
  • How you get credited. Your code credits you when someone uses it at checkout. Your link is any GoalIQ page with your code on the end of it, and we send you yours once the code is set up; it credits you a different way, by tagging the account at sign-up. That tag is read from the browser the link was opened in, so opening it on one phone and signing up on another won't count. That's why the sign-up count on your dashboard is a floor: the real number can only be higher. If someone's missing, tell us and we'll check it by hand.
  • Web payments only. App Store and Google Play subscriptions don't go through our checkout, so we can't see where they came from. That's been true since the program started, it just hasn't come up before. Send people to the site.
  • Commission follows completed payments. If a subscription is refunded or charged back, the commission on it doesn't stand.
  • Your own subscription doesn't count.
  • The rates above are for the season pass. The code also works on the 3.99 euro monthly plan. If someone uses it there, we'll agree the commission with you before it is paid.
  • Either of us can stop. Tell us and we'll retire the code. Anything already earned still gets paid.
