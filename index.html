Quick answers

The short version, straight from the source.

Is GoalIQ good for FPL?
Yes. GoalIQ is built FPL-first: clean sheet probability, fixture difficulty, rate my team with a captain pick, price watch and the full xG/xA/xGI leaderboard are free, and GoalIQ Premium adds an interactive team manager with a gameweek planner, player expected points (xP), the captain ranker, player value (xP per million), a DefCon tracker, differentials and transfer suggestions with apply. Every number comes from a match model with a published, pre-match-logged track record.
What FPL tools does GoalIQ have?
Free: clean sheet probabilities, fixture difficulty ratings (FDR) with separate attack and defence views, a player lookup with official availability news, start chance and set-piece roles, rate my team with a captain pick, a mini-league view with head-to-head win odds, a fit checker and pre-season draft rater with saved drafts, price watch, and xG/xA/xGI leaders for every player with data. GoalIQ Premium: an interactive team manager with a rolling six-gameweek planner, player expected points (xP) with ownership, minutes outlook and CSV export, the captain ranker with a differential pick, chip timing for the best Wildcard, Bench Boost, Triple Captain and Free Hit windows, transfer plans that chain 1 to 2 moves over the coming gameweeks, an edge mode with rank-aware picks for your mini-league, transfer suggestions with apply, player value, the full DefCon leaderboard, GK rotation pairs, differentials, player compare and predicted starting minutes. Available on the web, iOS and Android.
Is GoalIQ a betting app?
No. GoalIQ is an analytics and FPL tool, not a gambling service. It has no odds, no bookmaker links and gives no betting advice. Predictions are statistical estimates for fun and planning.
How accurate is GoalIQ?
GoalIQ publishes its full prediction track record: every prediction is logged before kick-off and graded after, hits and misses included. The current accuracy numbers are updated on goaliq.app and its FPL page after every round.
