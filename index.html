▌ Built by someone who kept missing the deadline

GoalIQ is made by one person. I have played Fantasy Premier League since 2012/13, and the record is public: 12 seasons, anyone can check it. My best finish is 450,207 (2020/21). My worst is 6,138,376 (2021/22), the season I stopped updating my team in October. One season I did not play at all.

That is the problem GoalIQ solves. Not beating the people who spend five hours a week on this. Just getting a defensible team in before the deadline, every week, without giving up your evening.

Every one of those seasons was played without the model. 2026/27 is the first one I use it myself, and I will publish how it went in May, including if it is worse.

▌ Quick answersP107
Is GoalIQ good for FPL?
Yes. GoalIQ is built FPL-first: clean sheet probability, fixture difficulty, rate my team with a captain pick, price watch with shareable image cards and the full xG/xA/xGI leaderboard are free, and GoalIQ Premium adds an interactive team manager with a gameweek planner, player expected points (xP), the captain ranker, player value (xP per million) for the top 50 with position and team filters, showing the scoring rate per 90 minutes and expected minutes separately so a high rate on low minutes reads as a bench risk and not a bargain, a DefCon tracker with price-band filters, differentials, shareable image cards (web) and transfer suggestions with apply. Every number comes from a match model with a published, pre-match-logged track record.
What FPL tools does GoalIQ have?
Free: clean sheet probabilities, fixture difficulty ratings (FDR) with separate attack and defence views, a player lookup with official availability news, start chance and set-piece roles, rate my team with a captain pick, a mini-league view with head-to-head win odds, a fit checker and pre-season draft rater with saved drafts, price watch, shareable image cards of the clean sheet outlook and price watch tables (web), xG/xA/xGI leaders for every player with data, and a filterable player stats table (shots, shots on target, shots in the box, headed attempts, non-penalty xG, key passes, tackles, clearances, recoveries and set-piece order) with per 90 and per start views and CSV export, and defence profiles showing what each team concedes by pitch zone. GoalIQ Premium: an interactive team manager with a rolling six-gameweek planner, player expected points (xP) with ownership, minutes outlook, the scoring rate per 90 minutes shown separately from expected minutes and CSV export (web), the captain ranker with a differential pick, chip timing for the best Wildcard, Bench Boost, Triple Captain and Free Hit windows, transfer plans that chain 1 to 2 moves over the coming gameweeks, an edge mode with rank-aware picks for your mini-league, transfer suggestions with apply, player value, a live DefCon panel that tracks your own squad against the threshold while a gameweek is being played (web), the full DefCon leaderboard, price-band filters with the model's xP on both leaderboards, shareable image cards of your XI, the captain ranker, the value ranking and leaderboards (web), GK rotation pairs, differentials, player compare for up to four players, and predicted starting minutes. The web app also predicts any match: win probability, expected goals, the most likely scorelines, upcoming fixtures and league tables. Available on the web, iOS and Android.
Is GoalIQ a betting app?
No. GoalIQ is an analytics and FPL tool, not a gambling service. It has no odds, no bookmaker links and gives no betting advice. Predictions are statistical estimates for fun and planning.
How accurate is GoalIQ?
GoalIQ publishes its full prediction track record: every prediction is logged before kick-off and graded after, hits and misses included. The current accuracy numbers are updated on goaliq.app and its FPL page after every round.
